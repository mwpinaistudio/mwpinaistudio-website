Privacy Policy

Your data, explained.

This privacy policy describes how MW PinAI Studio applications handle user information. For privacy questions, please contact the support email listed below.

Information we process

When users choose product photos or videos, those files are used only to provide product information and listing assistance. Users may edit, save, or remove their content. Do not upload content you do not have permission to use.

Third-party AI services

Product images, videos, and related text may be sent to third-party AI providers used by the application for recognition and content assistance. Data handling is subject to those providers' terms and privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Users can delete saved products and media from the application. Server-side records, when used, are retained only as long as needed to provide the service and support data deletion requests.
